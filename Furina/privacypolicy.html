Privacy Policy for Furina

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Furina Discord bot collects, processes, stores, and protects user data.

1. Information Collected

The bot may process the following information:

  • Discord User ID
  • Messages sent to the bot
  • Recent conversation history required to maintain short-term context
  • Technical information necessary for operation (such as rate-limiting data)

The bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

2. How Data Is Used

Collected information is used solely to:

  • Generate AI responses.
  • Maintain short-term conversation context.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and excessive requests.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the bot's reliability and stability.

3. Data Storage

To maintain conversation context, the bot stores a limited amount of recent conversation history.

The current implementation:

  • Stores up to 10 conversation entries per user (including both user and assistant messages).
  • Automatically removes older conversation entries when this limit is exceeded.
  • Stores conversation history locally in an AES-256-GCM encrypted database.
  • Does not store Discord User IDs directly. Instead, user identifiers are stored as SHA-256 hashes derived from the Discord User ID and a server-side secret key.

These implementation details may change in future versions of the bot.

4. Third-Party Services

User messages may be transmitted to third-party AI providers, such as Groq, solely for generating AI responses.

These providers process requests according to their own privacy policies. The bot developer does not control how third-party services handle data once it has been transmitted to them.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or intentionally disclose user data to third parties. Data may only be shared when:

  • Required for AI response generation through authorized AI providers.
  • Required to comply with applicable laws or legal obligations.

6. Data Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect stored data, including:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption for locally stored conversation history.
  • Hashed user identifiers.
  • Access restricted to the bot's runtime environment.

While reasonable efforts are made to secure data, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Conversation history is retained only as long as necessary for the bot's functionality. Data may be removed:

  • Automatically when the conversation history exceeds the configured storage limit.
  • Manually by the developer.
  • If the local database is reset or deleted.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the bot after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

9. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Service should be directed through the bot's official support server.
