Privacy Policy for Furina

Last Updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Furina Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, processes, stores, uses, and protects information when you interact with the Bot.

1. Information Collected

The Bot may process the following information:

  • Discord User ID (used only to generate an irreversible internal identifier).
  • Messages sent to the Bot.
  • Recent conversation history required to maintain short-term conversational context.
  • Technical information necessary for operation, including temporary rate-limiting data.

The Bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

2. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used solely to:

  • Generate AI responses.
  • Maintain short-term conversation context.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, and excessive requests.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Bot.
  • Diagnose and resolve technical issues.

3. Data Storage

To provide contextual AI conversations, the Bot stores a limited amount of recent conversation history.

The current implementation:

  • Stores only a limited amount of recent conversation history.
  • Automatically trims stored conversation history when it exceeds the configured context limit.
  • Stores conversation history locally using AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Never stores Discord User IDs directly.
  • Instead, the Bot stores an irreversible SHA-256 hash derived from the Discord User ID together with a server-side secret.
  • Stored data is accessible only by the Bot's runtime environment.

These implementation details may change in future versions of the Bot.

4. Third-Party Services

User messages are transmitted to Groq solely for the purpose of generating AI responses.

Once transmitted, information is processed according to Groq's own privacy practices. The Bot developer does not control how third-party services process information after transmission.

Only the information necessary to generate a response is transmitted.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or intentionally disclose user information to third parties.

Information may only be shared when:

  • Required for AI response generation through authorized AI providers.
  • Required to comply with applicable laws or legal obligations.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored information, including:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption for locally stored conversation history.
  • SHA-256 hashing of Discord User IDs before storage.
  • Restricted access to stored data.
  • Limited retention of conversation history.

While reasonable efforts are made to protect information, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Conversation history is retained only as long as necessary for the Bot's functionality.

Stored information may be removed:

  • Automatically when the configured conversation limit is exceeded.
  • Manually by the developer.
  • If the local database is reset, deleted, or replaced.

8. Rate Limiting

Temporary rate-limiting information may be processed to protect the Bot from spam, abuse, and excessive requests.

This information is used solely for abuse prevention and is not used for advertising or user profiling.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time.

The "Last Updated" date will be revised whenever significant changes are made.

Continued use of the Bot after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Service should be directed through the Bot's official support server or other official communication channels provided by the developer.
