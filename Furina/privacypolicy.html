Privacy Policy for Furina

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Furina bot handles user data.

1. Information Collected

The bot may process:

  • Discord User ID
  • Messages sent to the bot
  • Short-term conversation history required to maintain context
  • Technical information required for operation (such as rate limiting)

2. How Data Is Used

Collected information is used solely to:

  • Generate AI responses.
  • Maintain conversation context.
  • Prevent spam and abuse.
  • Improve stability and reliability.

3. Data Storage

Conversation history may be stored locally in an encrypted database for contextual memory. The bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

User messages may be sent to third-party AI providers (such as Groq) solely for generating responses. These providers process requests according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell or intentionally share user data with third parties except when required for the operation of the AI service or when required by law.

6. Data Security

Reasonable security measures, including encrypted local storage where applicable, are used to help protect stored data. However, no storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Conversation history may be retained for a limited period or until it is no longer required for the bot's functionality.

8. Contact

Questions regarding these Terms or Privacy Policy should be directed to the bot developer through the official support server.
